Why this exists

Three things hospitals still do blind.

  1. 01

    Crowded OPD halls

    Patients arrive early and stand in line for hours. Tokens and live waits let them wait anywhere and arrive near their turn.

  2. 02

    No view of free beds

    Nobody knows which ward has space. Bed boards and an admission waiting list fill the next clean bed in priority order.

  3. 03

    Pharmacy that runs out unnoticed

    Stock hits zero or expires on the shelf. Prescriptions handed over complete or not at all, plus reorder and expiry flags, keep inventory honest.

    Pick a hospital and department. You get a token like CAR-007.

  2. Wait anywhere

  3. Get called

    When the doctor calls you, the screen tells you which room.

  4. Seen

    The token closes. If the doctor prescribes, it goes straight to the pharmacy counter.
